Our Story

Founded on a belief in what art can do

HBICC was established in 2015 by Deepthi Krishna and her husband Sreejith Nair to promote Indian classical dance, arts, and cultural activities, including theatrical works — encouraging talent and shaping young dancers across the region.

What began as a small dance program grew into a full cultural institution, producing large-scale stage dramas and launching an annual charity event that continues to give back to Hawke's Bay.

What We Teach & Perform

Four disciplines, one stage

From the temple traditions of classical dance to the drama of the modern stage — every pillar is taught, rehearsed and performed at HBICC.

Dance

Classical Indian dance forms taught from first steps to stage-ready performance, shaping young dancers year after year.

Music

Drama

The People Behind HBICC

A volunteer-run team, all in on the arts

Eight people, drawn from dance, drama, music, healthcare and craft, keep HBICC's stages, classes and charity events running each year.

Artistic Director

Deepthi Krishna

Co-founded HBICC with her husband Sreejith Nair in 2015. A trained classical dancer who teaches HBICC's dance program, started the annual MADE for a Cause fundraiser, and founded Krishni Productions to create Shakuntala, Sita Unsung and Takshaka. Has staged nearly 60 performances across the East Coast, including one at Parliament in Wellington.

Chairperson

Ken Keys

A long-standing figure in Hawke's Bay theatre. Founded the Hawke's Bay Drama School in 1991 (now the National Youth Drama School) and Hawke's Bay Youth Theatre in 2003, staging over 50 productions and an Edinburgh Fringe tour. Directed all three of HBICC's dance-dramas.

Treasurer & Co-Founder

Sreejith Nair

Co-founded HBICC and serves as production manager for its annual events. Trained in dialysis therapy and currently works as a clinical physiologist with Health New Zealand Hawke's Bay.

Secretary

Sreedevi Sunil

An artist whose strength lies in drawing, design and costume craft. Moved to New Zealand from India, and her artwork and costume designs feature across most of HBICC's productions.

Choreographer

Sarada Naidu

A trained classical dancer and retired resource teacher who has been with HBICC since its earliest days, leading choreography and costume design for its shows, including Kathak and Odissi performances at past MADE events.

Bandleader & Composer

Anton Wuts

A composer, saxophonist and fixture of the Hawke's Bay jazz scene who wrote and performed music for The Hooligan and the Lady and part of the score for Takshaka. Founded Dr. Wuts & The Bindaas Bollywood Band in 2023.

Playwright

Pauline Ellen Hayes

An English and drama teacher whose company, Cookplace Productions, tells stories about overlooked women. Wrote HBICC's Shakuntala in collaboration with Deepthi Krishna.

Company Member

Aby Thomas

An actor and art lover who moved to New Zealand from India and has supported HBICC's initiatives since its founding. Lives in Napier and manages the recycling facility for Hastings District Council.
